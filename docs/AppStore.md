## Description

Rim turns the edge of your screen into a guide for your breath. A soft light travels the frame, one side as you breathe in, another as you hold, another as you breathe out, while haptics swell and settle with you. Close your eyes and you can still follow along.

Patterns for how you want to feel
• Steady: box breathing, 4 · 4 · 4 · 4
• Sleep: 4 · 7 · 8
• Balance: slow, even resonance breathing
• Reset: the physiological sigh
• Unwind, Triangle, Alternate nostril and Energize
• Power: cyclic hyperventilation and retention, with a hold timer
• Your own, set with simple dials

Urge
When a craving hits, one tap starts three sighs and a few minutes of long, slow exhales, with grounding prompts while it passes.

Made to be felt
• Haptics for every phase, and soft tones that keep going with the screen locked
• Hold to begin, tap to pause
• A gradient that breathes with you
• Sessions saved to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes
• Heart rate during sessions with AirPods Pro 3
• Widgets, Control Center, Shortcuts and a Live Activity
• A breath-hold test and a quiet history

Rim is not a medical device. Sit or lie down, never practise in water or while driving, and stop if you feel light-headed.
